<objective>
Implement tolerant step decorators and reporting-preserving soft-failure behavior.

Purpose: Users can mark assertion-like steps as tolerant while retaining accurate failed-step evidence in reports when failures are ignored at scenario outcome level.
Output: Public `@tolerant`, tolerant status policy, runner soft-failure handling, and integration/message tests.
</objective>

<scope_justification>
This plan intentionally owns 12 files because tolerant behavior must be implemented as one vertical slice over the same public decorator, definition metadata, runner status policy, and reporter-observable failure path. Splitting metadata from runner/reporting behavior would leave `@tolerant` importable but semantically inert, or would change runner/reporting behavior without a public way to mark tolerant steps. The write set is bounded to five step API/export files, four pickle-runner policy/lifecycle files, and three focused unit/integration/message test files.
</scope_justification>
