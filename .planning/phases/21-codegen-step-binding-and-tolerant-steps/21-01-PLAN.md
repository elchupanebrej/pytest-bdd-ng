<interfaces>
Existing codegen entrypoint registers --generate-missing, --generate, and --feature in `pytest_addoption`, then delegates `pytest_cmdline_main(config)` to `CodeGeneratorPlugin.pytest_cmdline_main(config)`.

Existing `CodeGeneratorPlugin` uses `wrap_session(config=config, doit=...)`, calls `pytest_collection(session=session)`, then `process_session_items(session)`, `collect_features_and_seen_uris(config, seen_feature_pickles_ids)`, `find_non_seen_features_and_pickles(...)`, and `find_unique_non_matched_steps(...)`.

Existing collection result shapes:
- `seen_feature_pickles_ids: set[tuple[str, str]]`
- `non_matched_feature_pickle_steps: list[tuple[tuple[FeatureRuntimeBinding, Pickle], PickleStep]]`
- missing scenarios: `list[tuple[FeatureRuntimeBinding, Pickle]]`
</interfaces>
</context>
